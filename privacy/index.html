Privacy Policy

Your audio never leaves your device. The free preview needs no account; Google sign-in is used only to buy Pro or unlock Pro features.

1. Data controller

This Privacy Policy applies to Songbench (referred to as "we", "us" or "the site"), accessible at https://songbench.online. Songbench is a project owned and operated by an independent developer; there is no separate legal entity. For any privacy or data-protection matter, contact contact@songbench.online. This policy was last updated on 2026-11-25.

2. What we collect - and what we do not

Core principle: Songbench is a fully client-side audio tool. Audio files are decoded, processed and downloaded on your own device. Our servers never receive your audio, so we cannot see, store or share it.

We do not collect:

  • No name, account or password for the free tools - they need no registration at all;
  • No audio files, file names or any metadata from your files;
  • No advertising identifiers, no browser fingerprinting, no analytics and no advertising scripts;
  • No third-party fonts or CDN calls for free usage (the Google sign-in script loads only when you buy Pro or request a Pro feature);
  • No IP-based user profiling by this site;
  • No practice-library contents - saved setups stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us.

We receive your information only in these cases:

  • When you email us at contact@songbench.online, we receive your address and message so we can reply;
  • When you buy Pro or use a Pro feature, you must sign in with Google. We receive and verify your Google ID token and see your email address, name and Google account identifier (sub). We use these only to keep you signed in, to attach a verified email to the Waffo order, and to ask Waffo whether a completed, non-refunded Pro order exists for that address. Anonymous checkout is not possible. We do not create a user profile or database record, send marketing email, or share this data with advertisers;
  • When you buy Songbench Pro, payment is handled by Waffo Pancake; we never receive or store your full card number;
  • Our hosting provider (Cloudflare Pages) may keep standard web-server access logs (requested URL, IP address, browser type) for security and delivery. This is the host's data practice, governed by its own policy.

3. How we use information

  • Customer support: only to answer the email you send us;
  • Identity and entitlement: to confirm a real Google account before a purchase or Pro feature, attach a verified email to the Waffo order, and check whether a completed, non-refunded Pro order belongs to that account;
  • Legal compliance and security: to respond to lawful requests and prevent abuse;
  • We do not use any information for advertising or marketing.

Because audio and processing happen locally, we need no personal data to "provide the service".

4. Cookies, local storage and tracking

For free and trial usage this site sets no cookies at all and deploys no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Opening a tool makes no background network request to our servers.

Practice library (browser local storage). If you save a practice setup, its settings - title, optional student or class label, file name, speed, key, tempo, loop points, named sections, effects and your note - are written to your browser's local storage (localStorage). This is not a cookie and is never transmitted to our servers: we cannot read it and keep no copy. Audio files are never stored. The data stays in that one browser until you delete it with Clear all, or until you clear site data, browse privately, or change device. Export backup writes a JSON file you control; Import backup restores it. Please do not enter sensitive personal information in the free-text fields. Automatic tempo and key estimates, beat grids, speed ladders and ZIP practice packs are computed locally and create no server-side record.

If, and only if, you buy Pro or use a Pro feature and sign in with Google, we set three first-party cookies: sb_session (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) holding Google's signed ID token, sb_signed (a non-personal flag used to know whether to check the session), and sb_pro (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax). The first two expire within one hour; sb_pro lasts up to one year and all three are removed by the Sign out button.

Pro entitlement cookie. sb_pro contains a cryptographically signed ticket holding your email address, a Waffo order identifier and issue/expiry times. It holds no card data, no audio and no password. To produce it, our server queries the Waffo order API for a completed one-time order for this product that has not been refunded and matches your verified Google email. We store no copy of that order and maintain no licence database. The ticket is re-checked against Waffo at least once every 24 hours, so a refund automatically ends Pro access. Signing in with the same Google account on another device recreates it - this is the "Restore Pro" function.

Google Identity Services is loaded from Google only at that moment; its own data practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy. No cookie is used for advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, including the "sale" as defined under laws such as the CCPA. We share information only in these cases:

  • Payment processor: card data is handled exclusively by the PCI-DSS-compliant processor Waffo Pancake and never touches our servers;
  • Identity provider: the Google ID token produced at sign-in is sent only to Google's tokeninfo endpoint for verification; we do not forward it to any other party. A verified email may be passed to Waffo Pancake to prefill a checkout you started, or used in a server-side query for your own completed orders when you activate or restore Pro;
  • Hosting and infrastructure: the site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, subject to its privacy policy and data-processing terms;
  • Legal requirements: disclosure under law, court order or a legitimate request from an authority;
  • Business transfers: in a merger or acquisition, with prior notice and continued protection obligations;
  • With your consent: for any other purpose with your explicit prior consent.

6. Data security

  • Transport encryption: HTTPS / TLS enforced site-wide;
  • Local processing: audio is not uploaded, so there is no server-side audio breach risk;
  • Payment isolation: card data is processed by Waffo Pancake; we store no card numbers;
  • Support email: stored in our mailbox, used only to reply, and deleted when the thread closes.

If a security event affecting your rights occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authority within the timeframe required by applicable law (referring to a 72-hour window where applicable).

7. Retention

  • Support email: deleted after the thread is closed, not retained long-term;
  • Google sign-in: the ID token lives only in your browser cookie for at most one hour. We keep no server-side session store, no account table and no login history;
  • Pro entitlement ticket: a signed cookie in your browser for at most one year, re-verified against Waffo daily. We keep no server-side licence record; Sign out removes it;
  • Payment records: retained by Waffo Pancake under its compliance obligations; we store none;
  • Practice library: held only in your browser's local storage, under your control. We hold no copy and cannot restore it - export a backup before clearing site data or switching devices;
  • Hosting access logs: handled by Cloudflare under its standard retention.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, object to processing and withdraw consent. To exercise them, email contact@songbench.online and we will respond within 30 calendar days. If you believe we have not handled a request properly, you may complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

The service is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from younger children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Third-party links and services

The site may link to third parties (for example the Waffo checkout or social platforms). This policy covers only information we collect directly; for third-party data practices, review their policies before use.

11. Changes

If we make material changes, we will give at least 15 days' notice via an on-page notice or the email used at purchase, and update the "last updated" date above. Continued use after the effective date means acceptance.

12. Contact

For privacy and data-protection matters, contact contact@songbench.online. This mailbox is monitored and is the point of contact for access, deletion and other privacy requests. The related terms are in the Terms of Service.

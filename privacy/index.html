Privacy policy

Your audio never leaves your device. There is no account, no upload and no tracking.

The short version

Songbench is a static site. Every tool runs in your browser with the Web Audio API. The audio files you open are decoded locally, processed locally and downloaded locally. We have no server that receives them, so we cannot see, store or share them.

What is not collected

  • No name, email address, account or password
  • No audio, no metadata from your files, no export history
  • No cookies, no local advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting
  • No analytics, no advertising scripts, no third-party fonts or CDNs
  • No IP-based profiling by this site

Share links

A shared practice sheet stores speed, key, loop points, tempo and notes in the URL fragment (the part after #). Fragments are not sent to web servers in an HTTP request. No audio is ever included in a share link.

Hosting

Your hosting provider may keep standard web-server access logs (requested URL, IP address, user agent) for security and delivery. This site itself sets no cookies and makes no background network calls.

Rights to the audio

Songbench does not supply songs, lyrics or downloads of third-party content. Only open audio you have the right to use. Slowing or adding reverb to a copyrighted recording does not make it royalty-free.

Contact email

The contact address for this site is contact@songbench.online. When you write to it, your message and your email address are delivered to the site operators mailbox so we can reply. We use that information only to answer your question, and we do not add it to a mailing list or share it with third parties. Messages are deleted once the thread is closed.

Changes

Last updated 2026-11-13. If a future version adds analytics, advertising or an account system, this page will be updated before those features ship.

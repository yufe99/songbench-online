How Change key of a song for my voice works here

This page is built around a specific rehearsal situation rather than a feature name, so the starting settings differ from the generic tool page. Every page on this site loads one shared workbench: you open a single audio file you have the rights to use, set speed, key, loop points and effects once, then take a free 60-second preview. Pro unlocks full-length and batch exports from that same file.

Where this page starts

No preset is applied: speed 1.00x, 0 semitones, reverb off, center cancellation off and output gain at 0 dB. You can change any of those values before exporting; the preset is a starting point, not a limit.

The exact steps on this page

In practice, move the Transpose slider in small semitone steps while listening to the chorus, then export at that setting and save the value in the practice sheet. The result is rendered with OfflineAudioContext and encoded to WAV inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded and the free preview needs no account; Google sign-in is required only to buy Pro or unlock Pro features.

What comes out

The free tier exports a 16-bit WAV preview of the first 60 seconds or of the A-B region; Pro exports the full track. Batch export writes several versions plus a plain-text manifest, and the share link carries speed, key, loop points and notes - never the audio.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account, and are my files uploaded?

No for the free tier. Audio is decoded and processed inside your browser with no upload and no watermark. A verified Google sign-in is required to buy Pro and to unlock Pro features, so the licence can be tied to your email.

Can I use copyrighted songs?

Only open audio you have the right to use: your own recordings, licensed material or student work. We do not provide songs, lyrics, or downloads from streaming or video platforms.

What exactly do I share with a student?

A practice sheet - speed, key shift, loop points, tempo and your note - encoded in the link. No audio travels with it, so the student opens their own legally held copy of the song and gets your exact settings.

Does slowing a song change the key?

No. The speed control keeps the original pitch. Transposition is applied when you export, so the practice sheet and the WAV agree.

Which browsers and formats work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Any format your browser decodes (MP3, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG, FLAC). Exports are 16-bit WAV.

Where are my saved practice setups stored?

In this browser only, using local storage. Nothing is uploaded and we keep no copy. Audio files are never stored - reopen the same song to continue. Clearing site data removes the library, so export a JSON backup if you want to keep it or move it to another device.

Is the automatic tempo and key detection exact?

No. Tempo is estimated from the audio's energy envelope and key from a chroma analysis; both are labelled estimates and can be overridden by hand or with Tap tempo. Always check by ear before relying on them for a lesson.

How do I restore Pro on another device?

Sign in with the same Google account you used to buy Pro, then click "Already purchased? Restore Pro" under the pricing card. We verify the completed Waffo order on the server and unlock the features again. Your licence is not tied to a particular browser or audio file.

Why does the pitch change when I slow a song down?

That is the point of a slowed + reverb or nightcore remix: when you play a record slower, everything gets lower and heavier; faster makes it bright and high. Our remix presets let the pitch follow the speed on purpose. If you want to rehearse a part in the original key instead, switch Pitch to locked (practice) - both modes use the same file and the same local engine.

Is Pro copy protection or DRM?

No. All audio processing happens in your browser, so a determined person could modify the page script. Pro is a low-cost convenience unlock, not a technical restriction - the honest reason the one-time price is set at a level where buying is easier than working around it.

How do I make a slowed + reverb song?

Click the Slowed + reverb preset: 0.85x speed with the pitch following the speed for the classic slowed sound, a generated hall tail and a warm bass shelf. Preview, then export WAV. All of it runs in your browser.

How do I make a nightcore or sped up version?

Click Nightcore (1.25x) or Sped up (1.15x). The pitch rises with the speed, which is the bright nightcore sound. Fine-tune with the Speed slider from 50% to 125%.

Does the pitch change when I slow a song down?

In remix mode yes, in practice mode no. The remix presets let pitch follow speed like a tape; the Pitch button under Speed locks the original key for rehearsal.

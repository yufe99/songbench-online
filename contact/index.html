Contact Songbench

Email the studio directly. No account, no form to fill, no upload.

Email us

For a school or department licence, an LMS embed, a billing invoice, a payment question or a bug report, write to contact@songbench.online. We answer on business days.

What to send

  • The song name or a link, and what you want to do with it (slow it down, change the key, loop a section)
  • Anything about billing, the Waffo checkout or a school licence
  • Bug reports: your browser and the file type you tried

Songbench is a browser-local tool, so there is nothing to upload and we never receive your audio. Please do not attach audio files - tell us the song instead.

Before you write

  • Export or playback not working? See the FAQ first.
  • Test-mode payment or receipt question? See the thank-you page.
  • Privacy questions? Read the privacy policy.

Rights

We do not host a song catalogue or supply audio. Only open audio you have the right to use. Slowing or adding reverb to a copyrighted recording does not make it royalty-free.
